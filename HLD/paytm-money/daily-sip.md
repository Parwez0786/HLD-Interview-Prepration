# Daily / Monthly SIP Widgets — Funnel Numbers

If you want precise, believable numbers for a Paytm Money interview, **don’t invent them from Paytm’s total user base**. Public Paytm data does not give the exact internal SIP-widget impressions / clicks / conversions needed to substantiate your **35%** and **22%** figures.

Paytm Money publicly says it has **21M+ users**, while its advertising platform identifies about **200K actively investing users**. Those are different populations.

For your resume bullet, frame the calculation using the **actual product funnel**, not total Paytm users.

---

## Interview-ready example

Suppose your widget was exposed to **200,000 relevant Paytm Money investment users** during the measurement period:

| Metric | Before widget | After widget |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Users seeing SIP entry point | 200,000 | 200,000 |
| Users clicking SIP | 40,000 | 54,000 |
| **CTR** | **20%** | **27%** |
| Users starting SIP flow | 40,000 | 54,000 |
| Successful SIP registrations | 4,000 | 6,588 |
| **Conversion** (success / flow starters) | **10%** | **12.2%** |

Then:

**CTR**

```text
(27% − 20%) / 20% × 100 = 35%
```

**Conversion**

```text
(12.2% − 10%) / 10% × 100 = 22%
```

`54,000 × 12.2% = 6,588` successful SIPs after the widget. Keep the conversion denominator as **flow starters**, not a frozen 40,000, or an interviewer can catch the mismatch.

---

## So your explanation becomes

> We had roughly **2 lakh eligible investment users** in the measurement cohort.
>
> Before the widget, around **40,000** users clicked into the SIP flow, giving us a **20% CTR**. After introducing the Daily and Monthly SIP widgets, clicks increased to around **54,000** from a comparable 2 lakh-user cohort, taking CTR to **27%**. That’s a **35% relative improvement**.
>
> For conversion, successful SIP registrations increased from around **4,000 out of 40,000** flow entrants (**10%**) to approximately **6,588 out of 54,000** flow entrants (**12.2%**). That’s a **22% relative improvement**.

---

## Don’t treat these as production facts

These exact figures are a **constructed example**, not verified Paytm Money internal data.

Don’t tell an interviewer that **2 lakh / 40k / 54k / 6,588** were your real production numbers unless you actually have those analytics.

---

## A safer way to defend your resume

Say:

> **35% relative increase in CTR** and **22% relative increase in conversion**, based on **internal analytics**.

Then use your **real dashboard numbers** if asked.

Paytm Money has publicly emphasized SIP adoption — for example, it previously reported that **over 75% of its investors preferred SIPs** and that it registered **7,000+ SIPs/day** at that time. Use public stats only as market context, not as proof of *your* widget lift.

---

## If you have real funnel counts

Replace the example with:

- users who **saw** the SIP entry / widget
- users who **clicked**
- users who **started** the SIP flow
- users who **completed** SIP setup

Then the 35% / 22% math and the cross-question answers can be rebuilt from those numbers exactly.
